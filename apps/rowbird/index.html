What you can do

Design charts

  • Create colorwork charts up to 120 by 120 squares, with up to 12 colors
  • Use stitch symbols: knit, purl, KTBL, PTBL, yarn over, k2tog, ssk, slip and no stitch
  • Paint, select, paste, add or delete a row or column, undo and redo, zoom, and fit the chart to your screen
  • Copy and paste between patterns with colors carried over, and keep a motif library
  • Start each pattern with a main color and a starting color palette

Knit from your chart

  • Row-by-row tracking: finished rows in full color, upcoming rows muted
  • A yarn-style view and a flat chart view
  • Right-side and wrong-side row instructions

Keep track of materials

  • A library of yarn, needle and gauge sets, with an approximate finished size

Print and share

  • Print your chart or share it as a PDF

Your patterns stay on your iPad

RowBird has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your patterns anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
